1. Executive Summary

NexaEdge is a protocol design to aggregate idle smartphone compute into a permissionless, distributed edge AI inference network. Modern smartphones contain dedicated Neural Processing Units (NPUs) that sit idle for 95%+ of the day. NexaEdge proposes to harness this latent compute to serve AI inference tasks at sub-5ms latency, at near-zero marginal cost, with native GDPR compliance.

The global edge AI market is projected to reach $107B by 2028 (CAGR 19.2%). Today, no protocol exists that aggregates smartphone NPU compute at scale. NexaEdge is designed to be the first.

The project is at concept validation stage (Q2 2026). A working waitlist portal, node registration system, and browser-based heartbeat client have been deployed. 100,000,000 NEXA tokens have been minted on Solana but are not yet in public circulation.

2. The Problem

2.1 The Edge AI Compute Gap

AI inference demand is growing faster than centralized GPU supply. H100 spot prices fluctuate between $2–4/hour, and data center capacity is constrained by geography, power availability, and capital expenditure. Meanwhile:

  • 6.8 billion smartphones contain NPUs capable of running 1.8B–3.8B parameter SLMs
  • These devices are idle for 12–20 hours per day
  • They are distributed across every geography, close to end users
  • Their compute goes entirely unused by the broader AI ecosystem

3. The NexaEdge Protocol

Turn every idle smartphone into a verified edge compute node that earns NEXA tokens for executing AI inference tasks.

3.1 Three-Layer Architecture

3.2 Proof of Compute (PoC)

Every node must solve a cryptographic ML inference verification puzzle before claiming rewards. The PoC mechanism combines:

  • Hardware fingerprint — device-specific, non-spoofable identifier
  • ZK proof of inference execution — verifiable without revealing model weights
  • BFT cross-validation — minimum quorum of independent nodes required

3.3 Privacy Architecture

NexaEdge is designed to be GDPR-compliant by architecture, not by policy:

  • Inference executes locally — data never leaves the jurisdiction
  • WASM sandbox provides OS-level isolation
  • Zero-knowledge proofs validate work without exposing input data
  • Node operators earn rewards without seeing the content of tasks they execute

3.4 39°C Thermal Protocol

If device temperature reaches 39°C, the task queue pauses automatically and passive cooling is activated. This constraint is enforced at the WASM sandbox level and is not overridable by the node operator — ensuring institutional buyers can rely on hardware safety guarantees.

7. Technical Moat

NPU-Native Execution

Apple A17 Pro NPU delivers 35 TOPS. Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 delivers 45 TOPS. These hardware accelerators are purpose-built for the matrix operations used in SLM inference. NexaEdge targets these NPUs directly via Core ML (iOS) and NNAPI (Android) through the WASM runtime.

39°C Thermal Protocol

Institutional AI buyers require SLA guarantees. The 39°C thermal ceiling is a planned daemon-level constraint enforced at the WASM sandbox — not overridable by node operators. This is the supply-side durability guarantee that enables enterprise SLA commitments.

Solana Mobile Integration

Solana Seeker and Saga are Web3-native smartphones with hardware-level key storage (Seed Vault). NexaEdge is designed to integrate natively with these devices, enabling seamless node enrollment and on-device NEXA wallet management.

Geographic Density Advantage

6.8 billion smartphones exist in every city, neighborhood, and rural area on Earth. This geographic density is structurally impossible to replicate with datacenters. As edge AI adoption grows, proximity to end users becomes a competitive moat that NexaEdge is uniquely positioned to occupy.

9. Risk Factors

Technical Risk

WASM runtime performance on mobile NPUs is unproven at scale. SLM inference on device may exhibit higher latency than projected. Thermal management may limit sustained workloads on certain device classes.

Regulatory Risk

Cryptocurrency token distribution is subject to evolving securities law across jurisdictions. NEXA token classification may require legal restructuring before public distribution.

Adoption Risk

Node operator acquisition requires sufficient NEXA reward rates to justify battery and compute usage. AI buyer acquisition requires demonstrated reliability and SLA compliance before enterprise commitments.

Competition Risk

Well-funded protocols (Akash, Render, io.net) may pivot to mobile compute. Apple and Google may restrict WASM execution on mobile in future OS updates.

NexaEdge is at concept stage. All projections, timelines, and technical claims are design targets, not guarantees.
